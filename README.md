# 🚀 Launchpad IT: Digital Portfolio

Welcome to the Module 1 Sandbox environment! 

### Step 1: Edit your Portfolio
1. Press the `.` (period) key on your keyboard to open the web IDE.
2. Open the `src` folder, then open `data.json`.
3. Replace the template text with your own name, skills, and bio.
4. Click the Source Control icon on the left, type a message, and click **Commit & Push**.
5. Click the menu in the top left and select **Go to Repository** to come back here.

### Step 2: Deploy to the Cloud
Click the button below to instantly deploy your live website.
